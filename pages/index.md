## Introduction

Hello! My name is {{ first_name }}. I'm a PhD student at the University of Texas at
Austin currently studying computer architecture. My advisor is
[Dr. Lizy Kurian John](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=YPu9rWUAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao),
and my work is with the
[Laboratory of Computer Architecture](https://lca.ece.utexas.edu/). During
Spring/Summer 2026, I'm working with AMD as a research associate intern
developing scale-up/scale-out GPU systems.

I'm interested in developing exciting things in computer architecture, which
makes my research difficult to specify in concrete terms. I want my
dissertation to consist of very different kinds of projects without any kind
of throughline, because there are too many interesting things happening in
this field to narrow down on any single topic. However, my current projects are
focused on adding *new instructions* to hardware systems, which involves
connecting computer microarchitecture, processor/memory systems, software
optimization, compilers, and more together.

My [Master's thesis](/research/media/master-thesis.pdf) is (1) an exploration
of deep-learning compilers, (2) an L2-cache microarchitecture for reducing
duplicated values in lowered im2col convolution, and (3) an analysis of
distributed GPU systems evaluating LLM workloads, and was advised by
[Dr. Hui Zhao](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=7DVxrRoAAAAJ&hl=en) at
the University of North Texas. My
[Bachelor's thesis](/projects/media/senior-thesis.pdf) is a detailed
investigation of programming languages, syntax vs. semantics, and the
introduction of a custom programming language maximizing expressibility of any
programming problem, advised by
[Dr. Barrett Bryant](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=hMCK39sAAAAJ&hl=en).
